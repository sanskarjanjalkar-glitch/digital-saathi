National Digital Safety Helplines (India)

If you or someone in your family ever suspects digital fraud, immediately reach out to these official government resources:

National Cyber Crime Helpline: Dial 1930

Available 24x7 across India for financial fraud and online complaints. Calling within the first 1–2 hours allows authorities to freeze stolen funds at the bank level!

📞 Call 1930 Now

Official National Cybercrime Portal

File a formal report online from home, upload screenshots, and track investigation status.

🌐 Visit cybercrime.gov.in

Sanchar Saathi (DoT, Govt of India)

Block lost phones, trace stolen devices via CEIR, and verify how many SIM cards are registered in your name at sancharsaathi.gov.in.

📱 Visit Sanchar Saathi Portal
